# SemesterSync 1.4.0: Assignment Inbox

**Capture any task in seconds, without a syllabus.**

- **Inbox tab:** type a task the way you'd say it, like “Biology paper due Friday, 5 pages” or “Study calculus for 45 minutes tomorrow”. SemesterSync proposes the title, course, due date and time, type, estimated time, notes and priority. Check them, fix anything, and save.
- **Never guesses:** when a date could mean two days (“next Tuesday” on a Monday, “next week”), it asks. It only matches courses you have, and a task with no date or course waits in the Inbox until you fill it in.
- **Quick Add from anywhere** (Mac and Windows app): press ⌘⇧Space (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+Space (Windows) in any app, type the task, confirm, and you're back to what you were doing. Choose a different shortcut, or turn it off, in Settings.
- **Everywhere it should be:** saved tasks show up in Today, the Plan (with study time booked before they're due), reminders, calendar export, Insights and search, and sync to your other computers.
- **Organize:** filters for tasks that need attention or have no course, search, edit, complete, delete with undo, Move to course, and Plan today.
- **Safe:** deleting a course keeps the tasks you added (they move back to the Inbox), duplicates are caught before saving, and a task added from Quick Add is kept on disk until it's safely saved.
